# Source Catalog

This catalog points at publicly presented items. It is not a dump of the manuscripts.

## Rights and citation

- Author of manuscripts: Valerijs Černohajev (also V.A. Černohaev), ~1980–2007; d. 2019.
- Public collection: [The Černohajev Archive — collection](https://cernohajev.omeka.net/collections/show/1)
- Editorial / translation notices associated with the public presentation should be respected. Quote sparingly. Do not treat this lattice as a rights holder of the 1980–2007 pages.
- Secondary critical-edition PDFs exist on third-party sites (e.g. engineeringinfinitybook.com). Prefer the Omeka item pages as the stable index.
