# TOLC 8 Ethics & Zero-Harm Note

This archive operates under the non-bypassable TOLC 8 Living Mercy Gates.

- **Truth:** Do not convert manuscript claims into demonstrated physics. Label status explicitly.
- **Order / Safety:** No construction manuals, winding schedules-as-build, or chamber recipes.
- **Non-proliferation:** Do not reconstruct weapons-relevant pathways. Fusion discussion stays at publicly standard reaction-channel and magnet-field comparison level.
- **Compassion:** Treat the late author's family archive and translators as rights-bearing humans. Cite; do not scrape-and-rebrand the corpus.
- **Abundance & Service:** The only allowed downstream is cleaner understanding that might later inform *already public* fusion, magnet, or MHD research questions.
- **No recovered-craft operationalization:** Provenance stories stay in the catalog as *claims about the manuscripts*, not as engineering inputs.

Any contribution that turns this archive into a vehicle or weapons project will be rejected by PATSAGi deliberation.
