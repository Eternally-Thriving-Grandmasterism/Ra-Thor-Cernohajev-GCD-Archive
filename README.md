## Attribution (source corpus)

Original manuscripts: Valerijs Černohajev (family-preferred Latin transliteration; also V.A. Černohaev), Soviet-Latvian aerospace engineer, RKIIGA; manuscripts ~1980–2007; died 2019.

Public research presentation: [The Černohajev Archive](https://cernohajev.omeka.net/collections/show/1). Translation and editorial work associated with Gene Sticco, Natalja Černohajeva-Sticco, and collaborators. This lattice **points at** those sources; it does not replace them and does not claim ownership of the manuscripts.
