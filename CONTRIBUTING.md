# Contributing

Canonical contact: info@Rathor.ai

This is a **research archive**, not a product repo.

## Welcome

- New catalog rows with stable URLs and identifiers.
- Claim-register rows that quote a number, unit, and source item.
- Comparisons to published magnet / MHD / fusion / planetary-field data, with citations.
- Dimensional-consistency notes that state assumptions.

## Not welcome

- Build instructions or CAD for vehicles, chambers, or solenoid arrays.
- Weapons, targeting, or classified-program reconstruction.
- Unsourced assertions that GCD is confirmed physics.
- Wholesale pastes of the manuscripts or critical-edition PDFs.

## Process

Open an issue with the literature or claim template. PATSAGi / TOLC 8 review applies to anything that would change a claim status out of `unvalidated`.
